What is a Custom Server & Workstation?

A custom server or workstation is a system engineered for your exact workload rather than picked off a spec sheet. TrayakshAI selects the right balance of CPUs, GPUs, memory, storage and networking for the applications you actually run, from AI development and simulation to rendering, data analysis and high frequency trading, so every dollar goes to performance you will use.
We begin by analyzing your exact software stack and computational bottlenecks. Our engineering team drafts tailored system architectures, matches enterprise-grade components, builds and stress-tests every machine in our lab under 100% load for 72 hours, and pre-installs the exact Linux distribution, CUDA toolkits, and machine learning environments you request.
Optimal resource allocation with zero wasted expenditure on unnecessary components; custom closed-loop liquid cooling for whisper-quiet office operation; sustained peak clock frequencies without thermal throttling; and direct white-glove support from the engineers who built your system.
Maintaining signal integrity across dense multi-GPU PCIe Gen 5 topologies; designing acoustics suitable for non-datacenter desktop environments; preventing voltage ripple under sudden transient power spikes; and validating enterprise Linux driver stability across bleeding-edge processors.

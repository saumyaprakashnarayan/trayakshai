What Is Data Management?

Data Management is both the process of data capture and protection while also keeping data available for users, processes and workflows. Efficient data management helps manage costs and keeps data protected, secure and available.
High-performance data management orchestrates hot NVMe flash storage for real-time model training, parallel file systems for cluster computing, and resilient object stores for multi-petabyte archiving. Automated policy engines tier data dynamically without taking volumes offline.
Eliminates GPU starvation by supplying hundreds of gigabytes per second of sustained throughput, breaks down data silos across engineering teams, provides immutable ransomware protection, and drastically cuts total storage expenditure through automated tiering.
Exponential data sprawl from sensor feeds and LLM pre-training; metadata congestion when dealing with billions of small files; avoiding vendor lock-in with proprietary storage appliances; and maintaining synchronous cross-site disaster recovery without throughput penalties.
